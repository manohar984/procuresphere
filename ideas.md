# ProcureSphere 360 design brief

## Theme: Connected enterprise infrastructure

ProcureSphere 360 is designed as a premium procurement operating system visualized as living infrastructure. The page moves from quiet precision in a light enterprise canvas into a few deliberate dark cinematic moments, then returns to a fully connected sphere for conversion.

## Core principles

- **Precision before decoration:** geometry is clean, measured, and readable.
- **Connection before complexity:** every line, state, and transition explains a procurement relationship.
- **Light first, dark with purpose:** white/soft gray surfaces carry most of the journey; deep navy appears for governance and the final CTA.
- **Business language with visual proof:** concise copy is paired with workflow evidence rather than walls of text.

## Color philosophy

- Canvas `#F7F8FA`, surfaces `#FFFFFF`, soft panels `#EEF2F6`.
- Structural navy `#0F1B2D`; ink `#1A2433`; muted ink `#5B6B7F`; border `#E3E8EF`.
- Primary blue `#2456D6` is reserved for active paths, selected states, and conversion actions.
- Success `#0E8A5F`, attention `#B87912`, exception `#B63B31`.
- No neon, excessive gradients, HPE green, cyberpunk glow, or random abstract blobs.

## Layout paradigm

Use a wide editorial grid with generous left alignment, narrow eyebrow labels, oversized headlines, and a consistent relationship between narrative copy and an engineered visual. Cards are mostly squared with modest 16–22px radii; borders carry more hierarchy than shadows. The hero and final reveal use a two-column composition; complex moments collapse into vertical stories on mobile.

## Signature elements

- The **Procurement Sphere**: layered circular geometry, orbit rings, active flow paths, and labeled nodes.
- **State rails**: small uppercase workflow states with blue/green/amber/red status semantics.
- **Product frames**: navy side navigation and white information surfaces to make the abstract story tangible.
- **Audit stream**: dark timeline with fine grid lines and structured event metadata.

## Interaction and animation

Use scroll position to move the story from fragmented to connected, update progress bars and active steps, and shift the product frame through modules. Pointer movement adds restrained sphere parallax. Hover states lift buttons/cards slightly and move arrows, never bounce. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables rotation, orbiting, and long travel, retaining the full information hierarchy with fades and static diagrams.

## Typography and voice

Use Inter or a modern enterprise grotesk stack. Voice is direct, trustworthy, intelligent, and operational: “Requests become decisions. Decisions become control.” Avoid hype, unsupported security claims, fake proof, and technical implementation details.

## Brand mark

A flat sphere/network symbol made from two concentric circular forms and three blue connection nodes. It should remain legible at favicon size, use no baked text, and share the same mark in the header and project icon.
